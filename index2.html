Know Before You Go

Safari Rules & Essential Tips

1

Carry Your Original ID

The same Aadhaar/Voter ID/Passport submitted during booking must be produced at the gate. No exceptions.

2

Dress in Earth Tones

Avoid bright colours. Wear olive, khaki, or brown to blend with the forest and avoid startling wildlife.

3

Stay in Your Vehicle

Stepping out of a jeep or canter at any point inside the forest is strictly prohibited and punishable.

4

Silence is Wildlife's Currency

No loud music, no sudden shouts. Quieter safaris lead to far more tiger encounters.

5

Book 45–90 Days Ahead

Permits open 45 days in advance for day safaris, and 90 days for Dhikala Night Stay. Weekend slots fill within hours.

Best Seasons to Visit

☀️
Peak Tiger Season
March – June
Dry heat draws animals to waterholes. Best tiger visibility. All zones fully open. Book at least 60 days ahead.
🌿
Pleasant Winter
Mid-November – February
Cool, misty mornings. Excellent bird watching. All zones open. The most comfortable weather for families.
🌧️
Green Monsoon
July – Mid-November
Core zones closed. Jhirna and Dhela remain open. Lush, emerald forest and far fewer tourists.
