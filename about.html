Who We Are

Royal Bengal Tiger India is based in Ramnagar, Uttarakhand — the gateway town to Jim Corbett National Park, India's oldest national park and one of the last strongholds of the wild Bengal tiger. We provide safari booking assistance for the park, and we handle the full process of getting you into the forest: checking zone availability, confirming your safari, and filing your e-permit application with the forest office.

What We Do

  • Jeep Safari bookings across all ten zones — Dhikala, Bijrani, Garjiya, Dhela, Hathi Dagar, Durga Devi, Jhirna, Phato, Sitabani, and Pakhro. See the full zone guide for detail on each.
  • Canter Safari seats into the Dhikala core zone, for day visitors who don't have a night stay booked.
  • Elephant Safari experiences where seasonally available.
  • Dhikala Forest Rest House night stays, for travellers who want more than a day trip inside the core zone.
  • End-to-end e-permit filing with the Forest Department, so you're not navigating government paperwork on your own.

How We Work

Being based in Ramnagar means we track zone-wise availability and gate timings directly, not through a call centre somewhere else. Most of our communication — booking confirmations, permit copies, last-minute gate changes — happens over WhatsApp, because it's genuinely the fastest way for us to reach you and for you to reach us back. We quote our service charge upfront and pass through government entry fees and taxes exactly as the Forest Department sets them, with no hidden "convenience fees" folded into the total.

Our Approach to Wildlife

Jim Corbett exists because of decades of conservation work, and every safari we arrange follows the Forest Department's rules on vehicle limits, noise, and guide supervision — not as a formality, but because a quieter, more respectful safari genuinely makes for a better one. We never promise a tiger sighting; nobody honestly can. What we can promise is a safari run the right way, in the right zone for the season you're travelling in, with a registered guide and driver on every trip.

Where We Operate

Every zone of Jim Corbett, from the core-zone exclusivity of Dhikala to the year-round accessibility of Jhirna and Dhela. If you're planning something more elevated — a private jeep, a luxury resort stay, a multi-day itinerary — our Luxury Corbett Safari Package page walks through what that looks like.
