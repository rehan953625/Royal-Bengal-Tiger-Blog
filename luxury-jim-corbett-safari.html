A Sample Journey

Your Itinerary, Curated

Every trip is tailored to your dates and interests — here's how a typical four-day private safari comes together.

Day1

Delhi to Corbett, in Comfort

Your chauffeur collects you from Delhi airport or hotel by mid-morning. The private drive to Ramnagar takes roughly 5-6 hours, with a comfortable lunch stop en route. Check in, unwind, and settle in before your first safari briefing.

Day2

Morning & Evening Safari, Bijrani Zone

Two private jeep safaris with your dedicated naturalist, timed for the best light and the highest historical sighting rates. Afternoons are free for the resort's spa, pool, or riverside terrace.

Day3

Dhikala Night Stay (Optional)

Transfer into the core zone for a night at the Dhikala Forest Rest House — the grassland, the Ramganga river views, and an evening far from any other resort or town.

Day4

Final Safari & Private Return Transfer

One last morning safari, a relaxed late checkout, and your private vehicle returns you to Delhi — or onward to Agra or Jaipur, if you're combining Corbett with the Golden Triangle.

A Room Chosen for the View

Corbett's luxury landscape sits mostly along the Kosi river — heritage-style lodges, spa resorts, and boutique properties with forest views on one side and the water on the other. We match the property to your dates, budget tier, and preferred zone.

Spa & Wellness on Arrival

Many of our partner-tier properties offer a spa treatment after your first safari — genuinely useful after an early, dusty morning drive.

Multi-Cuisine, Dietary-Aware Dining

Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, halal, or kosher-style meals are confirmed with the resort ahead of your arrival — no surprises at the table.

Positioned for Your Zone

We choose the property closest to whichever safari zone your itinerary is built around, so mornings start with a short drive, not a long one.

Built for the International Traveller

Most of our luxury guests arrive from the US, UK, Germany, and Canada. Here's what we handle so the logistics never become your problem.

Airport Pickup, Not Just Hotel Pickup
Your driver meets you at Delhi International Airport arrivals, holding a name board — no need to arrange your own transfer into the city first.
Entry Visa Guidance
Most nationalities need an Indian e-Visa or visa arranged in advance. We'll share the current official application link once your dates are confirmed — requirements vary by passport, so we always point you to the official source.
WhatsApp Support, Any Time Zone
Questions at 2 AM your time are still answered promptly — we're used to coordinating across US, UK, and European time zones.
Travel Insurance Recommended
Not legally required, but strongly advised — particularly cover for trip cancellation and medical evacuation, given Corbett's rural location.
