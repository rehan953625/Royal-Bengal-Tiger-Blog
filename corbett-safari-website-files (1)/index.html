1. Overview of Corbett Tiger Reserve

Established in 1936 across the Himalayan foothills of Nainital and Pauri Garhwal districts in Uttarakhand, Corbett National Park is India's oldest protected forest reserve and the inaugural site of Project Tiger (1973). Spanning 1,288 sq. km of sal woodland, riverine grasslands (chaurs), and boulder-strewn streams (sots) along the Ramganga and Kosi rivers, the reserve shelters one of the highest densities of Royal Bengal tigers and wild Asian elephants in the country.

When you send your enquiry via our live calculator above, our local Ramnagar desk verifies slot availability, processes your permit request, assigns a well-maintained Gypsy with an experienced driver, and arranges resort pickup.

4. What Is Included in Your Package Tariff

Our package rates combine the reserve entry fee, private 4x4 Gypsy hire, driver allowance, resort pickup/drop in the Ramnagar-Dhikuli belt, and our agency service charge. Because a Gypsy is hired per vehicle (up to 6 seats), a group of 6 pays only ₹1,084 to ₹1,250 per person.

5. Wildlife & Birdlife You Can Spot

Alongside the Royal Bengal tiger and Asian elephant, visitors frequently spot leopards, sloth bears, spotted deer (chital), sambar, hog deer, barking deer, wild boar, golden jackals, mugger crocodiles, gharials, and over 600 species of birds including the great hornbill and Pallas's fish eagle.

6. Packing & Photography Guidelines

Pack windproof layers for cold winter mornings (November–February) and cotton scarves, hats, and sunscreen for summer drives (March–June). Telephoto lenses (200–400mm) are ideal; flash photography and drones are strictly prohibited.

7. How to Reach Ramnagar

Ramnagar is 250 km from New Delhi by road (via Moradabad–Kashipur) and has direct train connectivity from Old Delhi (Ranikhet Express & link trains to Ramnagar Station RMR).

8. Nearby Sightseeing in Ramnagar

Between morning and afternoon shifts, guests can visit Garjiya Devi Temple on the Kosi river, Kaladhungi Waterfall (~25 km), the heritage museum at Chhoti Haldwani, or Kosi Barrage for birdwatching.
